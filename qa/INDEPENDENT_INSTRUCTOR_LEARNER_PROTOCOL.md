# Independent instructional and learner study — OPEN
Date: 5 November 2026

A trial capture form is available within **Assessments**. It is intentionally local/anonymous and has no backend. Any submitted record is self-declared; the form cannot establish reviewer independence, authenticity, expertise, skill transfer or safety certification.

## Study method
1. Recruit instructors with relevant controls/automation experience and representative learners **independently from the development team**. Keep recruitment/consent and identity records only with the authorized research administrator, not in the public code repository or exported anonymous data.
2. Assign versioned educational scenarios: electrical fault isolation; start/stop interlocks; conveyor sorting; ladder timer/counter; virtual machine commissioning; damaged project recovery.
3. Prior to the exercise, state exactly which behavior is a **virtual teaching model** and which real-world behavior is not modeled. Do not expose learners to energized real hardware.
4. Record whether the task was completed without hints, correctness of root-cause diagnosis, observed modeled isolation behavior, time spent, usability score and anonymous observations. Use the in-app form to export structured evidence.
5. Instructor reviewers separately check technical correctness, unsafe pedagogical assumptions, assessment rubric consistency and accessibility; compare same version and scenario.
6. Have a designated independent reviewer audit source/provenance, signed observations, number of unique participants, learning outcomes and defects. Record remediation and re-test.

No participants, instructors or independent reviewers have been contacted or tested by this session. The formal gate is still **NOT PASSED**.
