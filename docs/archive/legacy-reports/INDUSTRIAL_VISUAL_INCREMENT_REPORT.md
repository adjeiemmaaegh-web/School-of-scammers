# Industrial 3D visual refinement

Enhanced the process-driven Three.js scene with roller assemblies, drive drum, motor gearbox, sensor posts and indicators, safety fence geometry, control enclosure, floor, lighting and equipment labels. All animations and indicators remain derived from `ProcessState`; the rendering component does not own a separate clock or alter PLC/machine state.

**Scope:** procedural visual engineering increment only. No imported third-party assets, physics-engine migration, or backend work. Safety fences are visual representations, not a certified protective design.

**Verification:** see build output. Real browser visual review and hardware performance remain unverified.
